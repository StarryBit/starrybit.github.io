ECHOPULSE

You are blind. The only way to see is to make noise.
But in the dark, you aren't alone.

Play on Roblox Join the Discord

An engine built to map
sound into sight.

Standard Roblox rendering is entirely disabled. To create ECHOPULSE, I engineered a highly optimized audio-raycasting framework that translates sound waves into visual data in real-time.

Every footstep, dropped item, and ambient noise fires hundreds of spatial raycasts, painting the environment for a fraction of a second before fading back to pure black.

Survive the Silence

It's not just a visual gimmick. The echolocation mechanic is tied directly to the core gameplay loop, forcing players to constantly balance information with survival.

Strategic Noise

Throw objects to map distant areas, or stay perfectly still to hide. Every sound you make is a calculated risk.

Responsive AI

The entities hunting you operate on the exact same audio system. If you can hear them, they can hear you.

Spatial Soundscape

Built with a meticulously designed 3D audio environment. Playing with headphones is strictly required to survive.
