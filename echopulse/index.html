ECHOPULSE

You are blind. The only way to see is to make noise. An immersive stealth-horror experience built entirely on a custom audio-raycasting engine.

Play on Roblox Read the Devlogs ↓

Bypassing Standard Rendering

Standard Roblox rendering is entirely disabled. To create the echolocation effect, I engineered a highly optimized framework that translates sound waves into visual data in real-time.

Every footstep, dropped item, and ambient noise fires hundreds of spatial raycasts, painting the environment for a fraction of a second before fading back to pure black.

Systems at Play

Strategic Noise

Throw objects to map distant areas or stay perfectly still to hide. Every sound you make is a calculated risk tied directly to the core gameplay loop.

Responsive AI

The entities hunting you operate on the exact same audio system. If your noise can reveal the map, it can also reveal your position.

Insane Optimization

Achieving this required heavy object pooling, caching, and spatial partitioning to ensure client frame rates stay buttery smooth.
