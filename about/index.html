About Me

Roblox developer of 10+ years applying systems-level architecture to game development.

I’m a Roblox developer with a deep passion for space and gaming. My journey began in 2016, and it has pushed me to apply a systems-level perspective to the problems we face on the platform, all to elevate the development experience for everyone.

It's challenging to find safe, reliable, and optimized solutions on Roblox. I created this space to provide plug-and-play systems, heavy optimization, thorough documentation, and continuous support, giving developers more time to focus on game design.

Currently, I am actively building my most ambitious project yet: ECHOPULSE. It is a stealth-horror game where "sound is sight." Through intense object pooling, caching, and spatial partitioning, players must create noise to see, but that very same noise alerts the entities lurking in the dark.
