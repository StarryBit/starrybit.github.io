About Me

Roblox developer with 10+ years of experience pushing the engine's limits and creating unique experiences.

I'm a Roblox developer with a deep passion for space and gaming. My journey began in 2016, experimenting with simple models, but that early creativity would keep me building on my skills as a developer for years to come.

I have been working to build my professional brand as a developer , pushing the engine's limits in unique ways, and to unify my identity and create a space for all my work to breathe free of individual platform limitations, I made starrybitdev.com.

I'm always looking for new technologies to apply to my existing and future work and welcome technical discussions and collaborations with other creators. Currently, I'm working on my most ambitious project yet, ECHOPULSE. It's a stealth-horror game where "sound is sight," and through intense optimizations, I have created a world rendered through nothing but orbs.
